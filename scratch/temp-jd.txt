At ABB, we help industries run leaner and cleaner—and every person here makes that happen. You’ll be empowered to lead, supported to grow, and proud of the impact we create together. Join us and help run what runs the world.

This Position reports to:

Principal Software Engineer

 

What we believe in
ABB’s Process Automation business area enables customers to operate some of the world’s largest and most complex industrial infrastructures, helping them outrun – leaner and cleaner.
We offer a broad range of automation, electrification and digital solutions for process, hybrid and maritime industries, including industry-specific integrated control and software as well as measurement and analytics solutions and services.

Your role and responsibilities

We’re looking for a strong UI Automation Engineer / Software Developer with hands-on experience in building scalable automation for modern cloud applications.

In this role, you’ll work on UI and API automation, improve quality feedback through CI/CD pipelines, and help drive a modern quality engineering approach using AI-assisted and agentic automation where it adds real value.

The work model for the role is: Hybrid 

You will be mainly accountable for:

Design, build, and maintain UI automation for critical product and user workflows

Develop and enhance UI/API automation frameworks

Drive end-to-end validation across product features and integrations

Improve automation stability, maintainability, and execution reliability

Troubleshoot automation failures, pipeline issues, and flaky tests

Contribute to performance-focused validation and test data / simulation readiness

Use AI-assisted and agent-based automation techniques to accelerate development, debugging, and test effectiveness

Collaborate with SMEs, developers, and cross-functional teams to automate the right business-critical flows

Qualifications for the role:

3–6 years of experience in software development / test automation

Strong coding experience in C#, JavaScript, or TypeScript

Hands-on experience with Selenium, Playwright, Protractor, or similar UI automation tools

Good experience in UI and API test development

Exposure to REST / SOAP web services testing

Good understanding of automation framework design, code quality, and CI/CD integration

Solid understanding of web protocols, application architecture, and debugging

Strong analytical, communication, and problem-solving skills

Experience with agentic automation or AI-assisted coding tools

Exposure to Microsoft Azure

Azure certifications

Experience working with distributed / global teams

More about us

ABB is a leading global technology company that energizes the transformation of society and industry to achieve a more productive, sustainable future. The Process Automation (PA) business area (>$6B revenue in 2021, 22,000 employees), automates, electrifies and digitalizes some of the most complex industrial infrastructures on this planet. Through its five divisions, it serves customers in the energy, process and hybrid industries – from hydrocarbons, chemicals, water, mining, minerals, pulp & paper to marine and ports, and many more. PA stands at the heart of some of the most important shifts in society, helping the energy-intense industries to safer, smarter and more sustainable operations to enable a prosperous, low-carbon society.


Building a cleaner, smarter future takes all kinds of minds: the curious, the courageous, and the creative. We welcome people from all backgrounds and experiences. 

Ready to make an impact? Apply today or visit www.abb.com to learn more about the impact of our solutions across the globe.
